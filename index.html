geonwoo-cc

Multilingual caption tool for the YouTube channel “오늘건우 (Onul Geonwoo)”

What it does

geonwoo-cc is a private command-line tool used only by the owner of the “오늘건우” channel, a Korean family baby vlog. It uploads hand-translated closed captions in 16 languages, positioned next to the Korean on-screen text, and sets localized titles, descriptions and tags on the owner’s own videos, so international and hard-of-hearing viewers can follow along.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is not distributed, sold or offered to other users. Sign-in uses Google OAuth 2.0 and access is limited to the owner’s own channel.
